About S.K. Chem Solutions

Rooted in trust.
Driven by quality.

S.K. Chem Solutions was established in 2017 as a trusted importer of premium raw materials from leading manufacturers in Germany, Korea, Malaysia, and China.

We supply the Rubber & Latex, Household & Personal Care, Food & Beverage, Pharmaceutical, Estate & Plantation, Coatings & Printing Ink, Industrial & Engineering, and Specialty Chemical industries — guided by strong ethics and a genuine commitment to community and environment.

Our Story

Built on
long-term partnership

S.K. Chem Solutions was established in 2017 as a B2B importer of premium chemical raw materials from the world's leading manufacturers — including BASF (Germany & China), LG Chem (Korea), Vance Group (Malaysia), and SK Pickglobal (Korea).

We supply six key industries across Sri Lanka: Rubber & Latex, Personal Care, Home Care, Food, Pharmaceutical, and Textile Washing — providing the raw materials that manufacturers need to produce market-ready, quality products.

Every supply relationship is built on reliability, technical documentation, and genuine care for our clients' success — not just a transaction.
